## How to Prepare Scrap Metal for Recycling

Preparing scrap metal before pickup or drop-off can make the process easier.

Start by separating different types of metal where practical. Remove obvious non-metal materials when appropriate, and keep smaller pieces together so they are easier to handle.

For appliances and larger items, consider their size, weight, and accessibility. Heavy materials may require additional planning before they can be moved safely.

When preparing metal from a property cleanout, keeping recyclable materials separate from general junk can also simplify the overall removal process.

## Metal From Appliances

Old appliances are another common source of recyclable metal.

Items such as washers, dryers, ovens, refrigerators, and other large appliances can contain steel, aluminum, copper, and other materials.

Because appliances can be large and heavy, homeowners should consider access points, stairs, doorways, and transportation before attempting to move them.

## Copper, Aluminum, Steel, and Brass

Not all scrap metal has the same characteristics.

Copper is commonly found in electrical wiring, plumbing components, and certain equipment. Aluminum can be found in items such as siding, frames, and appliances. Steel and iron are common in construction materials, appliances, tools, and furniture.

Brass may appear in plumbing fixtures, fittings, and other hardware.

Separating these materials can make sorting easier and may help determine which recycling option is appropriate.

## Is Scrap Metal Worth Recycling?

The value of scrap metal can change depending on the material, grade, weight, and current market conditions.

Copper and certain other non-ferrous metals may have different values from common steel or iron. Prices can also change over time, so current pricing should be confirmed with the recycling facility or service handling the material.

Rather than relying on an old price estimate, identify the metal and check current conditions before deciding how to handle a large quantity.

## Large Metal Loads and Pickup
